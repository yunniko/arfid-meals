## About

arfid-meals helps people with ARFID (Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake
Disorder) or food intolerances build meals around what they can't or
won't eat, rather than assuming they can eat anything. It combines a
database of raw ingredients and market products with nutrition and
allergen information, a database of meals built from those, and a
personal black/white list you can set at the level of a whole food
group, a specific food, or a specific way that food is prepared.

## The single most important thing on this page

**This Service does not know your medical history, and cannot verify
that any ingredient, product, or meal it shows you is actually safe for
you.** Nutrition and allergen data comes from public databases and, for
raw ingredients, from automated name-based matching (see "Where our data
comes from," below) — both can be incomplete or wrong. Before eating
anything based on what this Service shows you, **you must independently
check for yourself** that it is safe for your own condition, allergies,
and intolerances. This applies every time, not just the first time —
treat every page as a suggestion to verify, never as a guarantee.

## Where our data comes from

- **Raw ingredient data** (e.g. "chicken breast, raw") comes from the
  USDA FoodData Central database (US government, public domain). Its
  allergen and gluten/lactose flags are generated by matching keywords in
  the ingredient's name — reliable for a single named ingredient, but a
  best-effort match, not a laboratory test.
- **Branded product data** (e.g. a specific packaged product available in
  Czechia) comes from Open Food Facts, a crowdsourced, openly-licensed
  (Open Database License) database. Its allergen tags are declared by
  contributors and can be incomplete, even for products that obviously
  contain a given allergen — an empty allergen list on a product is not
  a guarantee it's free of that allergen.
- **Meals** are composed by the Service's operator from the ingredients
  and products above; their nutrition totals are calculated
  automatically from those components' own data and inherit the same
  limitations.

## Privacy — how your data is used

### What we collect

- **Your email address and password** (stored as a one-way hash, never
  in plain text), to operate your account.
- **Your black/white list** (the food groups, ingredients, products, and
  preparation notes you choose to exclude or include). This is
  self-declared information you choose to enter to make the Service
  useful to you — it is not a medical record, and the Service does not
  ask why you've excluded something. Depending on what you enter, this
  information could nonetheless reveal something about your health,
  religious practice, or other personal circumstances; we handle it with
  the same care as the rest of your account data and do not use it for
  anything beyond generating meal suggestions for you.
- **Meals you create**, if your account has been given that permission,
  including their name, description, ingredients, and any preparation
  steps or safety notes you write.
- **One authentication cookie**, readable only by our server
  (`httpOnly`), that keeps you signed in.
- **One cookie for your interface language** (English or Czech),
  containing nothing but a language code.
- **Standard web server request logs** (IP address, requested page,
  timestamp), kept only as long as needed for security and abuse
  investigation — never used for tracking, profiling, or advertising.

### What we don't do

- No analytics, no advertising trackers, no third-party scripts of any
  kind loaded into any page.
- We never sell, rent, or share your account data or your black/white
  list with anyone.

### How long we keep it

Your account and everything attached to it (your black/white list, and
any meals you've created) is kept for as long as your account exists.

**Known gap, disclosed rather than hidden:** the Service does not yet
have a self-service way to delete your account or export your data.
Until it does, contact info@julienika.cz to request either, and it will
be handled manually.

### Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or
delete your personal data, or to object to how it's used. Contact
info@julienika.cz for any request — see the known gap above regarding
deletion in the meantime.
